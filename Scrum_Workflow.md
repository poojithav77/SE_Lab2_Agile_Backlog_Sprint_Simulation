# SCRUM WORKFLOW

## 1. Product Backlog
Contains all project requirements and user stories.

## 2. Sprint Planning
The team selects important backlog items and defines the sprint goal.

## 3. Sprint Backlog
Contains the selected tasks that the team plans to complete during the sprint.

## 4. Sprint Execution
Developers work on the selected tasks.

## 5. Daily Scrum
A short meeting to discuss:
- What was completed?
- What will be done next?
- Are there any blockers?

## 6. Sprint Review
The team demonstrates completed work and receives feedback.

## 7. Sprint Retrospective
The team discusses what went well and what should be improved.

## Scrum Board Columns
- To Do
- In Progress
- Done

## Conclusion
Scrum provides an iterative and collaborative approach to software development.
